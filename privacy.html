Privacy at Stacks Radar

Stacks Radar is designed to give users control over information associated with their use of the platform.

When you connect a wallet, Stacks Radar may display blockchain information associated with the connected wallet, such as balances, assets, NFTs, transactions, and activity.

Information recorded on the Stacks blockchain may be publicly observable. Stacks Radar may use blockchain and ecosystem data to provide analytics and intelligence features.
