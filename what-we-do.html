WHAT WE DO

Discover

Stacks Radar helps users find projects, protocols, tokens, NFTs, contracts, wallets, and other activity across Stacks.

Understand

Blockchain activity can contain a lot of information. Stacks Radar organizes that information into clear and useful data.

Monitor

Stacks Radar monitors activity across the ecosystem and identifies changes that may be important to investigate.

Analyze

Users can examine transactions, wallets, assets, protocols, contracts, NFTs, DeFi activity, and network activity.

Track Changes

Stacks Radar is designed to detect changes such as unusual activity, large movements, new contracts, new projects, and changes in ecosystem activity.

Connect the Information

The goal is to connect different pieces of ecosystem information so users can move from a signal to the underlying activity and investigate it further.
